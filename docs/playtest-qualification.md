## Primary shipped gameplay promise

For eligible survival players, a non-bypass lethal hit enters Death's Door instead of immediately completing death: the player survives at a sentinel HP value with semantic zero health and an injury; further accepted damage can roll for final death. Injuries affect death risk, maximum HP, movement and arms; a short healing lock follows zero crossing. A player can inspect their own body or interact with another player and deliberately start hands-on treatment, which removes injuries over time **without consumable items**. Final deaths freeze a life recap, and current-life injury state must survive appropriate reconnect/clone paths without leaking into a new life. Source anchors: `RevivalManager.java` (damage, state, treatment and recap), `RevivalForgeEvents.java` (death, login/logout/clone, player interaction), `state/BodyState.java`, `network/BodyActionPacket.java`, `client/MendScreen.java`, and `records/LifeRecords.java`. This is a source contract, not a claim of verified real-client UX or pack behavior.

### Acquisition / recipe roots

- **No treatment consumable is required**: `RevivalManager.HANDS_ON_CARE` and `state/MaimType.defaultTreatment()` use `better_deaths_door:care`; `RevivalGameTests.treatmentRequiresStartAndCuresWithoutItems` checks empty inventory and care history. Access is via self body UI / right-clicking an eligible player and the explicit start-treatment packet (`RevivalForgeEvents.interact`, `RevivalManager.openBody/startTreatment`, `BodyActionPacket`). No crafting table, loot chest, or block is a prerequisite for care in this source.
- `InjuryItems.java` registers `better_deaths_door:balm` (stack 16) and `better_deaths_door:soocher` to preserve saved item IDs, explicitly not as care requirements. There are item models and names under `src/main/resources/assets/better_deaths_door/`, but **no recipe JSON or block registry under this source tree** for these items; do not count them as obtainable survival recipe roots without a separate pack-side source/recipe check. Direct `cureOldest` calls with those IDs in `RevivalGameTests.java` exercise historical record compatibility, not a crafting or item-consumption route. Pack-level recipe-root inventory must check installed data packs/KubeJS and distinguish optional historical IDs from required progression.
